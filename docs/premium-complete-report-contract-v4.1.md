# Premium Complete Report Contract — V4.1.1

## Status and purpose

This document is the canonical product and methodology contract for the V4.1.1
**Premium Complete** natal report. It defines the reader-facing promise,
interpretive boundaries, mandatory coverage, editorial standards, and premium
publication path. It does not prescribe an implementation, data model, or
generation framework.

Premium Complete is a complete natal reading for a reader who wants a coherent
psychological account of the chart, not an exhaustive, equal-weight inventory
of every calculable item. It is symbolic and reflective, not a scientific
assessment, diagnosis, biography, or prediction service.

## Core promise

Premium Complete guarantees all of the following when the input supports them:

- A hierarchical reading: the chart's most consequential structures lead, while
  required natal components remain covered in proportion to their real
  relevance.
- A psychologically legible account of how chart functions, tensions,
  capacities, and counterweights may interact.
- Explicit coverage of the required natal components listed below, without
  turning their coverage into a uniform sequence of mini-readings.
- Clear separation between natal architecture, present timing, technical
  evidence, and reader choice.
- A report whose interpretive claims remain traceable to authorised chart
  evidence and whose published text has completed the premium review path.

Completeness means required coverage, coherent integration, and accessible
auditability. It does not mean that every factor receives equal space, every
house receives a standalone chapter, or every calculation becomes prose.

## Natal house policy

### Placidus is canonical for natal psychology

For natal psychological interpretation, Placidus is the canonical house
system. Natal placements, life contexts, spatial emphasis, and house-led
psychological interpretation are articulated through Placidus when the birth
time and calculation are reliable.

Whole Sign is not a competing parallel natal interpretation. It must not be
used to create a second, equally authoritative house narrative, to re-rank
natal psychological emphasis, or to rescue a desired reading when it conflicts
with Placidus.

### Legitimate Whole Sign use

Whole Sign may remain where it is intrinsic to a named technique or its
configured traditional logic, including annual profections and any configured
Lots or topical-rulership techniques that inherently require sign/Whole-Sign
topology. Such use must be identified as technique-specific rather than
presented as a second natal-house interpretation.

Shared zodiacal factors—sign, aspect, dispositorship, dignity, reception,
sect, speed, and planetary condition—remain shared factors. They are not owned
by either house system.

### Reliability and unavailable Placidus

Placidus-led natal house interpretation requires a known local birth time and
must pass the existing quality gates for house and angle use. If declared time
uncertainty, sensitivity, or unavailable Placidus calculation makes a relevant
placement unreliable, the report must:

- withhold that house or angle as established natal evidence;
- explain the limitation once in direct reader language;
- avoid replacing the withheld Placidus reading with a parallel Whole-Sign
  natal reading; and
- retain only the safe, non-house natal material and any technique-specific
  Whole-Sign material that is itself permitted by the applicable reliability
  gate.

The report may disclose conditional alternatives in optional technical depth;
it must not present them as settled psychological facts. Placidus agreement
with another classification is a robustness note, never an additional evidence
vote.

## Mandatory natal coverage

Coverage is mandatory; verbosity is adaptive. Each required component must be
interpreted or explicitly and briefly accounted for through its role in a
larger structure. A component need not receive a standalone heading when that
would repeat an already-explained mechanism.

Subject to the relevant reliability gates, Premium Complete covers:

- Sun;
- Moon;
- Mercury;
- Venus;
- Mars;
- Jupiter;
- Saturn;
- Uranus;
- Neptune;
- Pluto;
- Ascendant, when reliable;
- chart ruler, when reliable; and
- natal lunar nodes.

Coverage answers the reader's practical question—what role does this factor
play in the chart's architecture?—rather than merely naming sign, house, and
aspect. Modern outer planets may provide a central anchor only when their
connection is genuinely personal or angular enough to support that role; they
are never central merely because they are generational or numerous.

## Major structures and configurations

Promoted sign stelliums, Placidus-house concentrations, and major planetary
configurations are explicit interpretive obligations. A structure is promoted
only when it is safely calculated and materially relevant to the chart's
architecture.

- Sign stelliums and Placidus-house concentrations are integrated structures,
  not separate votes. When they describe the same body group, explain the group
  once and distinguish sign-function from Placidus-life-context only where the
  distinction adds meaning.
- Major configurations are explained once as a mechanism: the functions
  involved, their dynamism, likely pressure or capacity, material
  counterweights, and possible integration.
- Later planet coverage explains a planet's particular role in that mechanism;
  it does not restate the complete configuration.
- Important aspects are named when doing so helps orientation and are
  immediately translated into direct human language. Technical labels never
  substitute for explanation.

No structure earns attention merely because the software can detect it. The
same selection, confidence, relevance, and anti-double-counting standards
apply throughout.

## Hierarchy, selection, and composition

Premium Complete is complete **and** hierarchical. Selection controls emphasis,
sequence, connection, and depth; it does not erase the core natal components
required by this contract.

The reading remains governed by compositional semantics:

`planetary function × relational dynamic × natal context × modifiers ×
reliability → bounded symbolic proposition`

Interpretation therefore explains mechanisms before it inventories placements.
Every derived proposition stays within the authorised semantic ancestry of the
factors and claims supporting it. Valid contradictions may coexist or alternate
by context; the report must not flatten them into a falsely tidy personality.
Counterweights belong only when they materially qualify the proposition.

Completeness must not be achieved through canned planet × sign × house matrices,
large expansions of pair rules, or an equal-length paragraph for every body.
Those approaches reduce synthesis to catalogue prose and are incompatible with
this contract.

## Reader experience and voice

Predominantly address the reader directly in natural second person, while
varying sentence construction whenever repeated direct address would become
mechanical. The voice is psychologically legible, intimate, low-jargon, and
substantially more natural than V4.0.2. It speaks to observable patterns,
choices, sensitivities, resources, and contextual tensions without claiming to
know private history.

- Lead with interpretation and human meaning; introduce technical terms only
  when they clarify the mechanism.
- Central interpretive sections should normally lead with a psychologically
  recognisable pattern when that improves reader recognition, then reveal the
  astrology supporting it. Do not force this order when beginning with the
  astrological structure is clearer or more natural.
- Translate an aspect, configuration, sign, or house into ordinary language in
  the same interpretive movement.
- Develop leading dynamics into a recognisable mechanism, resource, tension or
  failure mode, material counterweight, and cross-factor interaction only where
  each element adds meaning; this is not a repeated prose template.
- Use concrete hypothetical examples only when they make a mechanism easier to
  recognise. They are invitations to test, not implied biographical facts.
- Avoid Barnum statements, guru language, moralised "shadow," and repeated
  disclaimers that make the report unreadable.
- Preserve a clear distinction between possible symbolic expression and a
  manifestation confirmed by the reader.

The Pattern may be used only as a high-level reference for readability,
progressive disclosure, and approachable psychological UX. Its wording,
templates, proprietary framing, and report structure must not be copied.

Interpretation comes first. Coaching, experiments, prompts, and questions are
secondary integration aids: useful after the reader understands the pattern,
never a substitute for the reading or a way to pad it.

## Report shape

Premium Complete uses progressive disclosure. Its exact section names and
lengths may adapt to the chart and locale, but its reader journey includes:

1. A clear opening architecture or a small set of distributed organising
   dynamics.
2. The most consequential patterns and major structures, explained once.
3. Integrated required natal coverage, with each body's role made clear and
   unnecessary repetition removed.
4. Placidus-led life contexts when reliable.
5. Current phase and development only when supported.
6. A synthesis with a small number of grounded questions or experiments.
7. Optional technical depth and an audit-oriented appendix.

The report has no hard word-count minimum. It is long enough to fulfil
mandatory coverage and explain the chart coherently, but it contracts where
the chart does not support more detail. Section length follows interpretive
importance and cognitive load, not a quota.

## Timing and developmental material

Timing activates natal architecture; it does not replace natal interpretation,
manufacture a new personality narrative, or predict inevitable events.

Where supported by the existing calculations, relevance/priority selection, and
reliability gates, Premium Complete may include the current phase, relevant
transits, annual profection, progressions, solar arcs, and existing
developmental or life-cycle intervals. Typed timing records are candidates, not
mandatory prose: material stays out of the reader flow when it cannot support a
useful, sufficiently specific human field linked to natal architecture.
The reader receives the human field first: what an activation may bring into
focus, what pressure or capacity it can amplify, and what remains unknowable.
Technical names, dates, streams, and convergence detail may be progressively
disclosed.

Dates are observation windows, not event promises. A derived cycle label is
not a second evidence source, and multiple techniques must not double-count the
same underlying phenomenon.

## Natal lunar-node axis

Premium Complete covers one natal lunar-node axis. The North Node is the
configured `true_node`; the South Node is its exact opposite point. The axis is
one structural evidence family, not two independent votes. Its two signs and,
when reliable, its two canonical Placidus houses are available together.

North Node language describes developmental direction and less-automatic
engagement; South Node language describes familiar, available patterning and
resources. Neither end describes destiny, mission, deficiency, or a pattern to
abandon. Relevant supported contacts qualify the axis once and must not be
double-counted through geometrically equivalent opposite-end descriptions. The
axis receives dedicated coverage but is not central merely because it is nodal.

## Technical appendix

The client-facing Technical Appendix makes the report useful without making the
main reading feel like a calculation dump. It is predominantly deterministic:
versions, input quality, positions, Placidus houses and their reliability,
angles, conditions, aspects/orbs, configurations, nodal axis, and selected
timing evidence belong there.

An internal audit sidecar retains policy disclosures, raw structured data,
claims, authorised syntheses, Chart Signature, Narrative Plan, evidence IDs,
and guard/run records. It remains auditable but is not part of the commercial
report. Neither artifact should consume expensive generated prose merely to
paraphrase the main reading.

## Premium publication contract

Premium Complete preserves the existing premium architecture:

`Author → Deterministic Provenance Guard → Reviewer/Editor → Publication Guard`

The Author creates one coherent draft from the closed, authorised factual
packet. The Deterministic Provenance Guard verifies allowed factors, semantic
ancestry, permitted operations, confidence ceilings, timing evidence, hashes,
and paragraph coverage. The Reviewer/Editor judges the writing as a fresh
reader: relevance, naturalness, repetition, meaningful counterweights,
preserved contradiction, and resistance to generic report swapping. The
Publication Guard releases only the exact approved text.

This contract adds no new agents, semantic judges, embeddings, databases, or
premium workflow stages. It preserves the existing division between
deterministic verification and human editorial judgment.

Configuration references may recur when a later synthesis adds a genuinely
different member role or level of analysis. A configuration and a same-member
sign/Placidus concentration group remain one structural evidence family: their
reuse cannot independently raise confidence, prominence, support counts, or
evidence-family counts, and their full structural explanation appears once.

## Hard safety boundaries

Premium Complete must not infer, diagnose, or promise:

- trauma, abuse, abandonment, mental or physical illness, or a diagnosis;
- death, pregnancy, divorce, bankruptcy, violence, or other concrete future
  events;
- private biography, identity, culture, gendered role, occupation, family
  history, relationship history, or hidden motive not voluntarily supplied by
  the reader; or
- certainty, causality, probability, or inevitability unsupported by the
  symbolic model.

Language stays calibrated: “may,” “can,” “tends to,” and equivalent language
describe symbolic possibilities, not statistical findings. Disagreement or
non-resonance is a valid reader outcome. Astrology does not replace clinical,
legal, financial, medical, therapeutic, or crisis support.

Localization may change language, units, dates, and culturally supplied
examples. It must never change natal evidence, interpretive weights, or the
specificity ceiling.

## Executive mode

Executive mode remains a distinct, compact reading. It delivers the essential
chart architecture, three to four genuinely supported dynamics, current phase
when supported, and one practical experiment or question. It is useful on its
own; it is not an abbreviated Premium Complete compliance report.

Executive mode may use the same safe facts and compositional standards, but it
does not guarantee the full mandatory coverage required of Premium Complete.

## Explicit non-goals

V4.1 does not:

- introduce a new astrology technique, factor, predictive system, or house
  calculation;
- make Whole Sign a second natal psychological house narrative;
- turn every planet, house, or aspect into an equal standalone section;
- expand pair-rule catalogues or generate canned placement matrices;
- add agents, semantic judges, embeddings, databases, or external model
  orchestration;
- treat deterministic fallback output as a premium report; or
- make the initial V4.1 run a benchmark result.

The initial V4.1 run is a low-cost human smoke test only. It may reveal product
or editorial issues for later review, but it is not benchmark evidence and must
not be used to fit the engine to an individual case.
