# Premium Complete Whole-Person Contract — V4.1.3

## Purpose

V4.1.3 defines the reader architecture of Premium Complete. It preserves the
V4.1.2 factual, semantic, reliability, timing, nodal, configuration, and
house-ruler foundations. It adds no astrology. Its product promise is fixed
human-life coverage with chart-earned depth, expressed as one connected reading.

## Canonical reader architecture

Premium Complete at `deep` depth has one fixed product preamble followed by exactly three interpretive layers:

0. the locale-bound deterministic reader introduction (`Como entrar nesta leitura`
   in Portuguese or `How to enter this reading` in English), reproduced verbatim
   once after an optional document title and before the opening; and

1. `Whole-Chart Opening`, explaining the organising architecture, leading
   mechanisms, material tensions, and connections that make the chart cohere;
2. exactly sixteen visible human domains, in the canonical order below; and
3. `Final Integration`, connecting recurring mechanisms across domains without
   repeating the reading or becoming generic coaching.

The opening and integration are not domains. Each owns at least one physical
narrative prose block with ordinary authorised provenance. Neither an empty
heading nor a table, list-only block, metadata, or deterministic notice counts as
narrative prose.

Localized headings are deterministic presentation values. The IDs and order are

hash cannot satisfy two sections.

Coverage is fixed; depth is earned by the chart. Low emphasis remains concise
but substantive; it is never a default one-paragraph rule and cannot suppress a
distinct authorised mechanism. No word count, paragraph quota, equal-depth rule,
or literary score determines compliance.

## Legal reader-routing paths

The deterministic domain manifest routes existing authority; it does not create
meaning. It may expose only these legal path kinds:

- `claim_anchored`: an existing Claim whose intrinsic semantic scope genuinely
  includes the human function being discussed;
- `topical_placidus`: reliable Placidus topical ancestry with authorised
  compositional context;
- `house_ruler_context`: the V4.1.2 non-scoring Placidus house-ruler Claim plus a
  canonically valid same-ruler context synthesis; and
- `timing_natal`: admitted typed timing composed with authorised natal ancestry.

Generic planetary existence never creates topical authority. Venus does not by
itself authorise money, Moon does not authorise family biography, Saturn does not
authorise vocation, and Pluto does not automatically authorise a shadow reading.
Broad headings do not broaden a path: each paragraph stays within the authorised
scope recorded for the path it cites.

Availability requires at least one legally constructible provenance path, not a
raw factor or a prebuilt thematic synthesis. Emphasis is only `high` or `low` and
is derived from membership in the frozen pre-domain `ChartSignature`; no domain
score is introduced. Unavailable means the closed packet genuinely lacks a legal
path. It never means merely low emphasis.

## Timing and unavailable domains

The active-life domain uses only admitted typed timing with explicit natal
ancestry. Its paragraph source row cites the exact timing IDs linked by the cited
synthesis. If no admissible path exists, the section contains the exact localized
notice supplied by the deterministic manifest. That notice is a non-literary
block: the Author cannot rewrite, extend, or use it as a route for interpretation.
The same rule applies to any other genuinely unavailable domain.

## Signature freeze and non-inflation

The canonical `ChartSignature` and its synthesis basis are prepared and hashed
before reader-domain routing. Domain coverage syntheses may remain available for
interpretation and provenance, but cannot recompute or alter anchors, structural
scores, core factors, theme priorities, prominence, confidence, support counts,
counterweights, contradictions, or evidence-family counts. House-ruler routing
remains contextual and non-scoring.

The original deterministic handoff is the Publication Guard authority. It binds
the packet identity, the prepared signature and synthesis basis, the reader
manifest, and one materialized effective `as_of` with its timing parameters.
Publication recomputation must match that handoff; a replacement set of mutually
consistent CLI inputs, provenance, and reviewer data is not an equivalent lineage.

## Reader introduction, narrative ownership and provenance

One canonical Premium narrative parser defines the physical block universe for
both section ownership and paragraph source-map validation. A single optional H1
document title may precede the fixed reader introduction. The introduction is
deterministic product copy, not astrology: its exact text is required, it cannot
be edited or extended, it receives no Claim or `ReasonedSynthesis` provenance,
and it never satisfies opening, domain, or integration coverage. No separator or
other prose may appear around it. The parser then recognises the exact opening,
sixteen domain headings, and final integration in canonical order; hashes ordinary
narrative paragraphs; and classifies deterministic notices separately. Headings,
tables, list-only blocks, and metadata are not prose blocks.

The Author and Reviewer declare section ownership using hashes from that same
universe. Guards verify exact headings and order, physical locality, exhaustive
and unique ownership, the canonical notice for unavailable sections, normal
source-map integrity, and intersection between cited synthesis ancestry and an
approved domain path. Direct atomic house-ruler Claims never satisfy substantive
domain coverage. The guards verify structure and non-literary ancestry; the
Reviewer judges whether the prose meaningfully answers the human question.

## Reader-selection completeness and regeneration

Premium handoff contract `1.3` adds a canonical internal `reader_selection_plan`
and its hash to the AuthorBundle, Provenance Guard output and ReviewerBundle.
It lists every available domain in manifest order and every legal coverage path
once, in manifest order. A path is either `represented` by a nonempty unique set
of individually approved syntheses found in that domain's physical provenance,
`merged_with_represented` into a direct same-domain represented target with a
nonempty rationale, or `omitted_no_distinct_reader_value` with a nonempty
rationale. Unavailable domains are absent. Unknown fields, missing, duplicate,
reordered and invented identifiers fail closed.

For a represented path and paths directly merged into it, the represented
synthesis set is checked in union against each legal path. Every member remains
individually valid and must contribute a required claim, factor, operation,
reasoning class or timing ID; unrelated padding fails. The union is a
provenance-only completeness check: it neither creates a synthetic synthesis nor
changes ordinary paragraph-local legality, ChartSignature, structural scoring,
or NarrativePlan. A timing cluster additionally has exact equality between its
union of synthesis timing IDs and its union of represented/merged path timing
IDs, while every individual timing paragraph keeps its existing exact row rule.

The Reviewer echoes the exact plan and hash. Its verdict is `approved`,
`regenerate_author`, or `blocked`. A regeneration request lists known unique
domain/path IDs and reasons; Publication Guard authenticates the prepared
handoff and plan before returning `author_regeneration_required` without a
report. Normal workflow allows one fresh Author regeneration from the original
handoff and one fresh Reviewer pass; a second regeneration request is blocked.

Layer A and Layer C require an approved relational synthesis using existing
integrated-pattern or theme-interaction composition authority. This is a
relational contract, not a factor-count heuristic. A house-ruler routing synthesis
alone does not satisfy it.

## Author and Reviewer

The Author speaks predominantly to the reader in varied, natural second person:
warm, receptive, grounded, psychologically sophisticated, non-mystical, and
non-diagnostic, without assuming grammatical gender. The preferred movement is human experience → astrology that
illuminates it → nuance or contradiction → reflection, without becoming a fixed
paragraph template. Human meaning normally leads when it improves recognition;
important astrology remains visible and is immediately translated. Interpretation
precedes coaching. The opening teaches central structures once; later domains add
every distinct, authorised human consequence rather than merely re-teaching the
astrology or deleting it for brevity. The final integration begins with the map’s
higher-order organisation, never with timeline bookkeeping, and develops rather
than simply summarises when several mechanisms remain distinct.

The fresh Reviewer protects both completeness and humanity. It expands an
underused available domain only from authorised packet evidence, compresses
repetition, rejects cookbook routing and chart-swappable claims, preserves real
contradictions, enforces direct-Claim atomicity, and removes unsupported biography,
certainty, timing, or coaching-led substitution.

## Safety boundaries

- Money concerns values, resources, security, risk, earning, and sharing as
  symbolic patterns; it supplies no financial advice or prediction.
- Body concerns energy, rhythm, routine, maintenance, and sustainability; it is
  not medical astrology and supplies no diagnosis, disease prediction, or
  treatment claim.
- Home and family never invent childhood, parent, or family biography.
- Shadow never diagnoses pathology or infers trauma.
- Developmental direction is not destiny, karma, mission, or inevitable progress.
- Current timing describes activated human fields, never inevitable events.

All prior prohibitions on inferred identity, biography, illness, concrete future
events, causality, certainty, and replacement of professional support remain in
force.
